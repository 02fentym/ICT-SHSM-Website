01 / IMAGINE

WHAT IS ICT SHSM?

Your interests.
A head start.

ICT SHSM is a Specialist High Skills Major for students who use technology to build things or provide a service. You combine school learning with practical experience, from creative work to coding and engineering. Everyone follows the same journey; your interests shape what you make along the way.
